Maren's direction is simple: **let anyone spend Bitcoin from their phone without selling it.**
The path there is ordered by safety, not by marketing. Nothing that touches other people's
Bitcoin reaches mainnet before an independent audit, and the pooled vault launches capped either
way.

<Info>
  This page states plans as plans. What already exists is on [What is real
  today](/get-started/introduction) and proven, transaction by transaction, in the repo. Everything
  below the current phase is planned, not shipped.
</Info>

## The phases

<Steps>
  <Step title="Phase 1: the engine (now, live on matsnet)">
    Five contracts live on Mezo testnet `31611`: pooled vault, payments, name registry, ERC-4626
    Earn, and the relay hub. The full loop runs end to end: deposit BTC, borrow from 0.001 BTC, pay
    a name, send a link, request money, and Earn. The web app is live against matsnet; the iOS app
    reads and writes live against those same contracts on matsnet, signing with a secp256k1 key held
    in the iOS Keychain behind Face ID. The borrow path has executed on-chain from the app, first on
    17 August 2026 (tx `0x28bb5ec1…7ef2`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Phase 2: beta and gasless (shipped, 2026-09)">
    The iOS app has a **TestFlight beta** invite on matsnet and the **gasless relayer is hosted** on
    Cloudflare. Eligible actions are sponsored while the service is available; deposits, approvals
    when needed, and link creation still require BTC. The public website serves landing, docs, and
    claims; its dashboard is intentionally disabled for the iOS-first release.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Phase 3: audit (the gate to mainnet)">
    An independent third-party audit including `MarenVault`, `MarenEarn` and `MarenRelayHub`, with
    findings and remediations published. No Maren contract reaches mainnet before this.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Phase 4: capped mainnet">
    Mainnet launch on chain `31612` with a hard-capped pooled vault. The cap is raised only as the
    position proves out, never on a schedule.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Phase 5: the real world">
    Money links polished for people who have never held crypto, bank cashout through licensed
    off-ramp partners in the first corridors, and a small merchant pilot settling in MUSD over
    Mezo's x402 rail. Design work, not code, today.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The vault cap rises on evidence

The pooled vault is the one component that can lose user funds through a Maren design failure, so
its cap is gated on proof, not time. See [Risk](/security/risk) for the full picture.

| Phase          | Cap     | Gate                                 |
| -------------- | ------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Mainnet launch | 5 BTC   | 14 days, no keeper failure           |
| Post-audit     | 25 BTC  | Critical and High findings resolved  |
| Post-traction  | 100 BTC | 60 days of clean liquidation history |

<Note>
  **If the team cannot satisfy itself the pooled vault is safe, Maren ships without it.** Direct
  troves, gasless payments, and Earn are valuable on their own. This commitment is binding.
</Note>

## How we will know it is working

Every measure of success is readable from the chain by anyone. The one that matters most is the
**spend ratio**: of the addresses that borrowed, the fraction that then spent what they borrowed.

| Metric                                      | What it shows                                            |
| ------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Funded positions below the protocol minimum | Borrowers who could not exist without Maren              |
| MUSD borrowed through the pooled vault      | New MUSD in circulation, attributable on-chain           |
| Payments settled against names and links    | The money-app claim, measured                            |
| Link claims by brand-new addresses          | The growth loop working                                  |
| Spend ratio                                 | Whether people spend Bitcoin, not just borrow against it |

<Info>
  Read live on matsnet on 2026-09-07, the pilot showed 3 funded positions, 2,110.72 MUSD borrowed,
  14 payments totalling 27.70 MUSD, 39.00 MUSD of Earn TVL across 3 depositors, and a spend ratio of
  **66.7% (2 of 3 borrowers have spent)**. These are testnet numbers from a handful of accounts,
  most of them ours — they show the loop works end to end, not that demand exists. Regenerate them
  with `pnpm --filter @maren/metrics start`. See [Verify on-chain](/developers/verify-on-chain).
</Info>
