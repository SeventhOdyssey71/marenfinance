Maren is a consumer borrowing and payments app for [Mezo](https://mezo.org). You deposit
Bitcoin, borrow MUSD against it from as little as 0.001 BTC, and spend it: pay a `@name`,
share a payment link, request money, and put idle MUSD to work. Your Bitcoin stays yours the
whole time, and when you repay you get all of it back. It is built for Mezo and deployed
nowhere else.

<Warning>
  **Maren is pre-audit and lives on Mezo testnet only.** Every contract runs on Mezo matsnet
  (chain `31611`). Maren's mainnet addresses are all zeroes because Maren is not on mainnet:
  a third-party audit is the deliberate gate. Do not use Maren with funds you cannot afford
  to lose. The relayer that makes actions gasless is built and tested but not yet hosted, so
  today your first deposit costs BTC gas.
</Warning>

## The honest one paragraph

Mezo already runs the hard part: a live, audited borrowing engine where BTC mints a dollar.
What it does not have is a consumer door. The protocol imposes a 2,000 MUSD minimum loan,
about $3,000 of BTC to open responsibly, so a small holder cannot borrow at all; every action
costs BTC gas; and once you hold MUSD there is almost nowhere to spend it. On-chain, MUSD is a
DeFi-internal asset: only about 4.6% of its supply was created by user borrowing, and fewer
than thirty troves are open. Maren is a bet that a consumer layer can create demand where none
exists yet. Its real edge is not cheaper collateral: it is **no minimum loan size** at all. You
can borrow $25 against $77 of Bitcoin, which the protocol itself cannot do. Whether meaningful
demand exists is unproven, and we treat it as a bet rather than a certainty.

## What Maren is not

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Not a claim of pent-up demand" icon="chart-line">
    We do not argue that thousands of idle MUSD holders are waiting to borrow. Borrowing on
    Mezo is barely used and whale-dominated. Maren is a demand-creation bet on a small, early
    chain, and the honest evidence for it is Mezo's off-chain community, not any on-chain
    count.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Not zero gas, yet" icon="gas-pump">
    The gasless path uses an ERC-2771 relayer that is built and fully tested but not hosted.
    Until it is live, you submit your own transactions and pay BTC gas. Even once it is live,
    the very first deposit into the vault always costs gas by design. See
    [Gasless](/how-it-works/gasless).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Not a savings account" icon="piggy-bank">
    Earn is an ERC-4626 wrapper over the MUSD Stability Pool. Its returns come from real
    liquidation gains, which are lumpy. Maren shows trailing realised yield and never
    advertises a forward APY, because there is no rate to guarantee.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
