Maren Finance

Spend Bitcoin. Never sell it. A money app for Bitcoin, built on Mezo.

Maren is a consumer borrowing and payments app for Mezo. You deposit Bitcoin, borrow MUSD against it from as little as 0.001 BTC, and spend it: pay a @name, share a payment link, request money, and put idle MUSD to work. Your Bitcoin stays yours the whole time, and when you repay you get all of it back. It is built for Mezo and deployed nowhere else.

Maren is pre-audit and lives on Mezo testnet only. Every contract runs on Mezo matsnet (chain 31611). Maren's mainnet addresses are all zeroes because Maren is not on mainnet: a third-party audit is the deliberate gate. Do not use Maren with funds you cannot afford to lose. The relayer that makes actions gasless is built and tested but not yet hosted, so today your first deposit costs BTC gas.

The honest one paragraph

Mezo already runs the hard part: a live, audited borrowing engine where BTC mints a dollar. What it does not have is a consumer door. The protocol imposes a 2,000 MUSD minimum loan, about $3,000 of BTC to open responsibly, so a small holder cannot borrow at all; every action costs BTC gas; and once you hold MUSD there is almost nowhere to spend it. On-chain, MUSD is a DeFi-internal asset: only about 4.6% of its supply was created by user borrowing, and fewer than thirty troves are open. Maren is a bet that a consumer layer can create demand where none exists yet. Its real edge is not cheaper collateral: it is no minimum loan size at all. You can borrow $25 against $77 of Bitcoin, which the protocol itself cannot do. Whether meaningful demand exists is unproven, and we treat it as a bet rather than a certainty.

What is real today

Live on matsnet
Five source-verified contracts on Mezo testnet 31611. Borrow, repay, names, payments, requests, and Earn all run against the real MUSD protocol.
Proven on-chain
A real user borrowed 25.7 MUSD through the app. The pooled trove is live inside Mezo's own TroveManager. Every claim carries a transaction hash.
Not yet mainnet
No mainnet deployment, no paid audit, and the relayer is not hosted. Each of these is stated on the pages that depend on it rather than buried.

What Maren is not

Not a claim of pent-up demand
We do not argue that thousands of idle MUSD holders are waiting to borrow. Borrowing on Mezo is barely used and whale-dominated. Maren is a demand-creation bet on a small, early chain, and the honest evidence for it is Mezo's off-chain community, not any on-chain count.
Not zero gas, yet
The gasless path uses an ERC-2771 relayer that is built and fully tested but not hosted. Until it is live, you submit your own transactions and pay BTC gas. Even once it is live, the very first deposit into the vault always costs gas by design. See Gasless.
Not a savings account
Earn is an ERC-4626 wrapper over the MUSD Stability Pool. Its returns come from real liquidation gains, which are lumpy. Maren shows trailing realised yield and never advertises a forward APY, because there is no rate to guarantee.
