Once you hold MUSD, Pay is how it moves. `MarenPay` operates on MUSD, takes no custody, and
moves money wallet to wallet under your own permit. It works for anyone holding MUSD, whether
they borrowed it or not.

## Pay a name

Money moves to `@names` instead of addresses. Type `@amaka`, an amount, an optional note, and
send. Raw addresses still work for anyone without a name yet.

Handles live in `MarenRegistry` and are registered through **commit-reveal** so a name cannot
be sniped from the mempool. A handle is 3 to 32 characters from `[a-z0-9_]`, one primary handle
per address, with reverse resolution for display.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Commit">
    Submit a hash of your desired handle plus a secret. Nobody watching the mempool can tell
    which name you are claiming.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reveal">
    Submit the handle and secret. The registry checks the commit and assigns the name. On
    matsnet, `@sele` was registered this way, with the reveal landing at 119,726 gas.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resolve">
    `resolve("sele")` returns the owner address on-chain. The payment interface always shows
    the resolved address alongside the handle before you confirm, so you see where money is
    actually going.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Restricting handles to `[a-z0-9_]` eliminates homoglyph attacks, and showing the resolved
  address before confirmation is the second line of defence. See [handle risk](/security/risk).
</Note>

## Money links

Put MUSD into a link and send the link anywhere text goes: WhatsApp, iMessage, anywhere. The
recipient opens it and claims the money, **with or without a Maren account and holding no BTC**.
Unclaimed links can be cancelled and refunded.

This is how money crosses from Maren users to people who have never held crypto, and it is the
product's built-in growth loop: every link is an invitation carrying money.

<Info>
  `createLink` / `claimLink` are built and exercised on testnet. Polishing money links for
  non-crypto recipients is Phase 3 work, planned rather than shipped. The pilot metric that
  matters here is **claims made by addresses brand new to Maren**, which is the growth loop
  working.
</Info>

## Requests

The reverse direction. Ask for an amount with a note, share it as a link, and the payer settles
it in two taps. Settled requests appear in both parties' [Activity](/product/activity) with the
transaction hash attached. On matsnet, a 0.50 MUSD payment request has been created on-chain
through the app.

## The full payment surface

`MarenPay` uses `ERC2771Context`, so its actions can be relayed gaslessly once the relayer is
hosted.

<ResponseField name="pay(to, amount, memo)" type="direct transfer">
  Move MUSD to an address with an on-chain memo event.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="payTo(handle, amount, memo)" type="pay a name">
  Resolve a `@handle` through the registry and pay it.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="createRequest / fulfillRequest" type="request money">
  Ask for an amount; the payer settles it.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="createLink / claimLink" type="money link">
  A claimable link the recipient opens with no prior relationship and no BTC.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="split(recipients[], amounts[], memo)" type="one to many">
  One signature paying many recipients.
</ResponseField>

<Warning>
  Cash out to a bank and pay at a shop are **planned, not built**. Cashout integrates licensed
  off-ramp partners rather than taking custody of fiat; merchant payments settle in MUSD over
  Mezo's x402 rail. Both are Phase 3 design work. Nothing in the app pretends otherwise.
</Warning>
