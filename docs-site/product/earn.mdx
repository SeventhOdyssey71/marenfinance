Idle MUSD can sit productively. `MarenEarn` is an ERC-4626 vault over MUSD's Stability Pool: it
provides your MUSD to the pool, sweeps the BTC liquidation gains the pool pays out, converts
them, and compounds them back into MUSD so `totalAssets()` grows for shareholders.

## Where the yield comes from

<Warning>
  The MUSD Stability Pool has **no token emissions**. Yield is liquidation gains only, which
  makes it lumpy and impossible to state as a fixed rate. Maren displays **trailing realised
  yield**, labelled as backward-looking, and refuses to advertise a forward APY. Any product
  presenting a forward APY here is presenting a number it invented.
</Warning>

Returns are real liquidation gains. When a trove is liquidated, Stability Pool depositors
absorb its debt and receive its collateral at a discount. That collateral is the yield. It
arrives in bursts tied to market events, not on a schedule, so there is no rate to promise.

<Note>
  Earn is a vault, not a savings account. The product's own iconography reflects this: Earn is
  drawn as a rising line and never a piggy bank, because the screen's disclosure says it is not
  a savings account and the icon must not contradict the copy.
</Note>

## The full cycle, proven on-chain

The Earn path is the whole product in three transactions, and all three are on matsnet:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Deposit Bitcoin">
    BTC deposited into the vault as collateral.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Borrow MUSD">
    40 MUSD drawn against it through the pooled vault.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place MUSD into Earn">
    25 MUSD placed into `MarenEarn`. MUSD's own Stability Pool then reports a 25 MUSD deposit
    for the Earn vault, confirmed by `getCompoundedMUSDDeposit`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Every layer of the stack touched the real protocol, and each step is a row in
[Activity](/product/activity) with its hash linked to the explorer.

## What Earn reports

<ResponseField name="totalAssets()" type="uint256">
  The vault's MUSD value including compounded liquidation gains. This is what grows over time,
  and it is the only honest way to express the return: realised, backward-looking.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="deposit / withdraw / redeem" type="ERC-4626">
  Standard ERC-4626 share accounting. Shares represent a proportional claim on `totalAssets()`.
</ResponseField>

<Info>
  There is a known open item on the Earn screen from the [conformance audit](/security/audits):
  an exposed staleness flag (`isUsingStalePrice`) that nothing currently reads. It is left open
  on purpose because it needs a designed "price unavailable" state in the Earn screen rather
  than a silent guard, and inventing that copy without deciding the intent would be guessing.
</Info>

## Revenue on Earn

The only fee Maren takes on Earn is a **performance fee: a share of realised liquidation gains
only**, targeted at 10% of realised gains. If liquidations yield nothing, there is no fee.
Nothing charges for holding.
